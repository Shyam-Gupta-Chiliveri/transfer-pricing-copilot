Findings 2025

HTPL net cost plus 2.00% is below the rangeManufacturing costs €8,000,000. Narrowed range 4.20%–6.80%, median 5.40%. Gap to the median: €272,000 more operating profit. § 1 (3a) AStG; OECD TPG paragraphs 2.64 and 3.60.
HTUS operating margin 10.17% is above the rangeDistributor range 2.10%–3.90%, median 3.00%. The routine distributor is keeping a residual that belongs to the principal.
A royalty does not sit with the contract manufacturerHTPL pays €80,000 of royalty to HTDE and manufactures only for HTDE. The royalty is its own transaction and must not hollow out the routine return. § 1 (3c) AStG.
Management charge with no named contractThe transaction matrix under § 90 (3) AO requires the contractual basis. The charge is booked. The contract field is empty.
